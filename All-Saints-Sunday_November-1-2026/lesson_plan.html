Sunday School Plan: All Saints' Sunday

All Saints' Sunday (White)
Scripture: Genesis 37–46 (Joseph)
Theme: God Is With Joseph, Getting to Know God, God Looks After Us
Big Idea: God wants us to know him.
Key Verse: "Come near to God, and he will come near to you." James 4:8a
Series: Know (Week 1)

Master Materials List (for about 10 children)

  • Offering plate
  • Laptop with the 3 videos (Desktop → Church → Joseph (Know Week 1) → Videos)
  • Wi-Fi for the song: "Coat of Many Colors" on YouTube
  • Something colourful to wear as "Joseph's coat": a bright scarf, beach towel, or blanket (optional)
  • Crowns: bulletin board border, cut into 24" strips (12), Key Verse heart cut-outs (12), heart stickers (about 10 per child), markers, masking tape, scissors, and a stapler
  • Spider-web chart: "How Does God Look After You?" page (1 copy for the whole class), a marker, and wall-safe tape
  • Grain Shake-Up: a play parachute and 20 yellow pom-poms
  • Take-home cards, 12 (3 sheets printed double-sided, colour if possible, cut apart)
  • Key Verse colouring pages, 10 (black and white)

Before Class (about 15 minutes)

  1. Cut out the 12 hearts and put a loop of masking tape on the back of each one.
  2. Cut the take-home sheets into 12 cards.
  3. Cut 12 crown strips, about 2 feet long each (two sheets of paper end to end is about right).
  4. On your laptop, open the Videos folder and open the song in your browser so it's ready.
  5. Set the craft table with the strips, hearts, stickers, markers, stapler, and tape.
  6. Put the parachute and pom-poms off to the side.

1. Introduction and Welcome

5 minutes: Weekend Check-In

Welcome everyone! Ask the children how their week was and let a few share. Then ask: "Does anyone have a favourite piece of clothing? What colour is it?" Let a few share. Then preview the lesson: "Today is a special day called All Saints' Sunday. It's when we remember all of God's people who got to know God and loved him. Today we are going to meet one of them. His name is Joseph! He had a super colourful coat, some amazing dreams, and a really big adventure. And God was with him the whole time! But first, let's do our offering."

2. Offering and Prayer

1 minute: Offering

Collect the offering/donations while singing the money dropping song.

1 minute: Prayer over the Offering

"God, thank you for being with us, just like you were with Joseph. We give you this gift today. Help us to get to know you more. In Jesus' name, Amen."

Transition: "Let's all sit on the carpet and hear a story from the Bible!"

3. The Story

5 minutes: Storytime Video

There's no flipbook this week. The video is the story: Simon reads it with pictures, like a picture book on the screen.

Video: Desktop → Church → Joseph (Know Week 1) → Videos → Storytime Joseph.mp4 (about 4 minutes)

"We're going to hear a story from the Bible now. Let's all get comfortable and watch the video together."

(Optional: before the video, put on your colourful "Joseph's coat" and tell the story yourself with these actions.)

"Joseph's dad, Jacob, had twelve sons! He loved Joseph so much that he gave him a beautiful coat with ALL the colours of the rainbow." [Pretend to put on a fancy coat, then spin around to show it off!]

"Joseph had dreams that one day his whole family would bow down to him. His big brothers did NOT like that. They were jealous and super grumpy!" [Cross your arms and make your grumpiest face!]

"The brothers threw Joseph into a deep hole and sold him to people who took him far away to a land called Egypt. Then they tricked their dad with Joseph's torn coat, so he thought Joseph was gone forever. Poor Jacob was so sad." [Rub your eyes and pretend to cry, "Boo-hoo!"]

"In Egypt, Joseph was put in jail even though he didn't do anything wrong. But God was with Joseph! One night the king had a strange dream, and God helped Joseph know what it meant: there would be 7 years of lots of food, then 7 years with no food at all. So the king put Joseph in charge, and Joseph filled giant barns with grain!" [Scoop up big armfuls of pretend grain and pile it up high!]

"When the hungry years came, Joseph's brothers came to Egypt to buy food. They didn't even know it was him! Was Joseph angry? NO! Joseph forgave them, gave them food, and hugged his whole family. God was with Joseph the whole time!" [Give yourself the biggest hug you can!]

4. Questions and Reflections

5 minutes: Ask the children the following questions:

  • What did Jacob give to Joseph to show his love for him? (Answer: A colourful coat!)
  • How did Joseph's brothers feel? Did they like him? (Answer: They were jealous and grumpy. No, they didn't!)
  • What did the brothers do to get rid of Joseph? (Answer: They threw him in a hole and sold him to people going to Egypt.)
  • How did God help Joseph? (Answer: God helped him understand the king's dream, so he could save food for everyone.)
  • When Joseph saw his brothers again, was he angry? (Answer: No! He forgave them and hugged them.)
  • Joseph got to know God, and God wants us to know him too! How can we get to know God? (Answer: Praying, hearing Bible stories, coming to church, singing to God.)

Transition: "Everybody stand up! Let's learn our Bible verse."

5. Key Verse

3 minutes: Key Verse Video and Actions

Video: Videos → Key Verse Video with Paige and Lauren Know Early Years.mp4 (about 1½ minutes). Have everyone stand and copy Paige and Lauren's actions. This is our Bible reading for today. Then say it together one more time:

"Come near to God, and he will come near to you." James 4:8

(No video? Use these actions: "Come near" [wave your hand toward you] "to God" [point up] "and he will come near" [hug yourself] "to you!" [point to a friend])

Transition: "Stay standing. Let's sing about Joseph's coat!"

6. Song

3 minutes: "Coat of Many Colors" (YouTube)

Play the song and dance together! (This one needs Wi-Fi.)

Transition: "When the king put Joseph in charge, Joseph got to dress like a king! Who wants to make a crown? Come to the table!"

7. Craft: Key Verse Crowns

15 minutes: Royal Crowns (Main Craft)

Why: Joseph wore royal clothes when the king put him in charge. The heart on the crown has our Key Verse, so it reminds the children that God is near.

Finished crown: a colourful paper band around the head, with the Key Verse heart on the front and stickers all over it.

(If you don't have bulletin board border, tape two strips of construction paper together to make one long strip. No stickers? Markers are fine!)

  1. Give each child a crown strip, a Key Verse heart, and some heart stickers.
  2. Put the markers in the middle of the table where everyone can reach.
  3. Hold up a heart and read the Key Verse out loud together: "Come near to God and he will come near to you."
  4. Have them stick their heart onto the middle of their crown strip.
  5. Decorate the crowns with heart stickers and markers.
  6. While they work, ask: "What does it mean to come near to God?" (Talking to God, praying, singing to him, learning about him!)
  7. As each child finishes, wrap the strip around their head to fit, then staple it closed.
  8. Put a piece of tape over the staple so it isn't poky!

Transition: "Finish your crowns, we've got a fun game coming! Now wear your crown and come sit with me on the carpet."

8. How Does God Look After You?

5 minutes: Spider-Web Chart

Why: God looked after Joseph, and God looks after us too. This helps the children see how.

  1. Sit on the floor together with the "How Does God Look After You?" page.
  2. Ask each child: "How does God look after you?" Give ideas if needed: food to eat, a family who loves me, a warm home, friends, keeping me safe.
  3. Draw a line out from the middle circle for each child, like a spider web. Write their answer and their name at the end (keep it short, like "Food – Sam").
  4. Tape it by the door so parents see it at pickup.

Transition: "God looked after Joseph by giving him lots of food to share with everyone. Let's play a game about that!"

9. Game: Grain Shake-Up

5–10 minutes: Parachute Game

Why: In the story, God told Joseph to save food for 7 good years, then share it when everyone was hungry. The parachute is Joseph's barn, and the pom-poms are the food.

(Nobody goes under the parachute. Everyone just holds the edge. Any children who don't want to play can colour a Key Verse colouring page.)

How to Play:

  1. Spread the parachute flat on the floor. Everyone stands around it and holds the edge with both hands.
  2. Practise together: "Up high!" (lift it up) and "Down low!" (back to the floor). Do it 2–3 times.
  3. Toss the pom-poms into the middle: "This is Joseph's barn, and this is the food!"
  4. The 7 Good Years: Shake it gently while everyone counts to 7 out loud. Try to keep the food in the barn!
  5. The Hungry Years: "Now everyone's hungry! Let's share the food!" Shake hard until ALL the pom-poms bounce off.
  6. Kids grab the pom-poms and throw them back on. Play again! There are no "outs."
  7. Fun ending: Lift the parachute up high together and call 2 children by name to run underneath and swap sides. Keep going until everyone has had a turn.

Transition: "Great job! Let's sit down and calm our bodies for prayer."

10. Closing Prayer

1 minute: Prayer Time Video

Video: Videos → Prayer Time God is Near Know Early Years.mp4 (about 1 minute). Children sit and follow along.

(No video? Lead it yourself:) "Let's give ourselves a big hug, so we can feel how near God is to us." [Wrap your arms around yourself] "Dear God, thank you for loving us and for being near to us, just like you were with Joseph. Help us to know you more and more. In Jesus' name, Amen."

11. Pickup

  • Have the children wear their crowns to meet their parents!
  • Take-home cards are for parents. The front tells them this month's stories (Joseph, Moses, David, and Daniel) and the Big Idea. The back has the Key Verse and 3 simple things to do at home this week, like reading a Bible story at bedtime.
  • Give each parent 1 take-home card and 1 Key Verse colouring page: "Today we learned about Joseph. Here's what to talk about at home this week!"

12. Extra Activities (Only If You Have Time)

  • Disciple Tag: You are "it." Run around and chase the children! Keep it gentle with soft tags, and no outs.
  • Joseph's Coat Parade: Let each child take a turn wearing the colourful "coat" and spinning around while everyone cheers, "God is with you!"
