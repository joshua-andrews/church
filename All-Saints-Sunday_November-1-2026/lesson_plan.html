Sunday School Plan: All Saints' Sunday

All Saints' Sunday (White)
Scripture: Genesis 37–46 (Joseph)
Theme: God Is With Joseph, Getting to Know God, God Looks After Us
Big Idea: God wants us to know him.
Key Verse: "Come near to God, and he will come near to you." James 4:8a
Series: Know (Week 1)

Master Materials List (for about 10 children)

  • Offering plate
  • Laptop with the 3 videos downloaded (Church folder on your Desktop → Joseph (Know Week 1) → Videos)
  • Something colourful to wear as "Joseph's coat": a bright scarf, beach towel, or blanket (optional, but the kids will love it!)
  • A Bible (bookmark Genesis 39 and Genesis 45)
  • Crowns: bulletin board border, cut into 24" strips (12), Key Verse heart cut-outs (12), heart stickers (about 10 per child), markers, masking tape, and a stapler
  • Grain Shake-Up: a play parachute (or a big bedsheet) and 20 yellow pom-poms (or scrunched-up balls of yellow paper)
  • "How Does God Look After You?" page (1 copy, 11×17), a marker, and wall-safe tape
  • Take-home cards, 12 (3 sheets printed double-sided in colour, cut apart)
  • Key Verse colouring pages, 10 (black and white)

1. Introduction and Welcome

5 minutes: Weekend Check-In

Welcome everyone! Ask the children how their week was and let a few share. Then ask: "Does anyone have a favourite piece of clothing? What colour is it?" Let a few share. Then preview the lesson: "Today is a special day called All Saints' Sunday. It's when we remember all of God's people who got to know God and loved him. Today we are going to meet one of them. His name is Joseph! He had a super colourful coat, some amazing dreams, and a really big adventure. And God was with him the whole time! But first, let's do our offering."

2. Offering and Prayer

1 minute: Offering

Collect the offering/donations while singing the money dropping song.

1 minute: Prayer over the Offering

"God, thank you for being with us, just like you were with Joseph. We give you this gift today. Help us to get to know you more. In Jesus' name, Amen."

3. The Story

5 minutes: Interactive Story

The Story: (Sit the children on the floor. Put on your colourful "Joseph's coat" before you start! Read this out loud and encourage them to act it out with you.)

"Joseph's dad, Jacob, had twelve sons! He loved Joseph so much that he gave him a beautiful coat with ALL the colours of the rainbow." [Pretend to put on a fancy coat, then spin around to show it off!]

"Joseph had dreams that one day his whole family would bow down to him. His big brothers did NOT like that. They were jealous and super grumpy!" [Cross your arms and make your grumpiest face!]

"The brothers threw Joseph into a deep hole and sold him to people who took him far away to a land called Egypt. Then they tricked their dad with Joseph's torn coat, so he thought Joseph was gone forever. Poor Jacob was so sad." [Rub your eyes and pretend to cry, "Boo-hoo!"]

"In Egypt, Joseph was put in jail even though he didn't do anything wrong. But God was with Joseph! One night the king had a strange dream, and God helped Joseph know what it meant: there would be 7 years of lots of food, then 7 years with no food at all. So the king put Joseph in charge, and Joseph filled giant barns with grain!" [Scoop up big armfuls of pretend grain and pile it up high!]

"When the hungry years came, Joseph's brothers came to Egypt to buy food. They didn't even know it was him! Was Joseph angry? NO! Joseph forgave them, gave them food, and hugged his whole family. God was with Joseph the whole time!" [Give yourself the biggest hug you can!]

Video: Play the 4-minute "Storytime: Joseph" video on the laptop. It's in the Church folder on your Desktop under Joseph (Know Week 1) → Videos → Storytime Joseph.mp4.

"Now let's hear Simon read us the story of Joseph! Let's all get comfortable and watch together."

4. Questions and Reflections

5 minutes: Ask the children the following questions:

  • What did Jacob give to Joseph to show his love for him? (Answer: A colourful coat!)
  • How did Joseph's brothers feel? Did they like him? (Answer: They were jealous and grumpy. No, they didn't!)
  • What did the brothers do to get rid of Joseph? (Answer: They threw him in a hole and sold him to people going to Egypt.)
  • How did God help Joseph? (Answer: God helped him understand the king's dream, so he could save food for everyone.)
  • When Joseph saw his brothers again, was he angry? (Answer: No! He forgave them and hugged them.)
  • Joseph got to know God, and God wants us to know him too! How can we get to know God? (Answer: Praying, hearing Bible stories, coming to church, singing to God.)

5. Key Verse

3 minutes: Key Verse Video and Actions

Play the Key Verse video (about 1½ minutes): Videos → Key Verse Video with Paige and Lauren. Have everyone stand and copy Paige and Lauren's actions. Then say it together one more time:

"Come near to God, and he will come near to you." James 4:8

(No video? Use these actions: "Come near" [wave your hand toward you] "to God" [point up] "and he will come near" [hug yourself] "to you!" [point to a friend])

6. Craft: Key Verse Crowns

15 minutes: Royal Crowns (Main Craft)

Transition: "When the king put Joseph in charge, Joseph got to wear fancy royal clothes! Let's make our very own royal crowns with our Key Verse on the front!"

(Before class: cut the bulletin board border into 24" strips, one per child. Print the hearts on white paper (2 hearts per page) and cut them out. Put a loop of masking tape on the back of each heart. If you don't have bulletin board border, tape two strips of construction paper together to make one long strip.)

  1. Give each child a crown strip, a Key Verse heart, and some heart stickers.
  2. Put the markers in the middle of the table where everyone can reach.
  3. Hold up a heart and read the Key Verse out loud together: "Come near to God and he will come near to you."
  4. Have them stick their heart onto the middle of their crown strip.
  5. Decorate the crowns with heart stickers and markers.
  6. While they work, ask: "What does it mean to come near to God?" (Talking to God, praying, singing to him, learning about him!)
  7. As each child finishes, wrap the strip around their head to fit, then staple it closed.
  8. Put a piece of tape over the staple so it isn't poky!

7. Reading the Scripture

2 minutes: Genesis 39:21 and 45:4–5

Transition: "Now let's read the real words from the Bible! Joseph's story is in the very first book of the Bible, called Genesis." (Hold up the Bible open to Genesis so the children can see it.)

"But the LORD was with Joseph and showed him steadfast love..." (Genesis 39:21)

"Then Joseph said to his brothers, 'Come closer to me.' And they came closer. He said, 'I am your brother, Joseph, whom you sold into Egypt. And now do not be distressed, or angry with yourselves, because you sold me here; for God sent me before you to preserve life.'" (Genesis 45:4–5)

Ask: "Did you hear what Joseph said to his brothers? 'Come closer!' That sounds just like our Key Verse: Come near to God, and he will come near to you!"

8. Game / Activity

5–10 minutes: Grain Shake-Up

(Note: Any children who don't want to participate can colour using the Key Verse colouring pages!)

(No parachute? Use a big bedsheet or tablecloth. No pom-poms? Scrunch up yellow paper into balls!)

Intro: "Remember how Joseph filled giant barns with grain so nobody would go hungry? This parachute is our barn, and these yellow pom-poms are our grain!"

How to Play:

  1. Spread the parachute on the floor and have everyone hold the edges. Play sitting down if the group is extra wiggly.
  2. Practise lifting it up high and bringing it down low together.
  3. The 7 Good Years: Toss the pom-poms onto the parachute. Gently shake it 7 times while everyone counts out loud, and try to keep the grain in the barn!
  4. The Hungry Years: Now the people are hungry! Shake hard and bounce ALL the grain off the parachute to share it with everyone.
  5. Collect the pom-poms and play again. There are no "outs"—everyone keeps playing!
  6. As you play, remind them: "God looked after Joseph, and God looks after us too!"

9. Wrap Up and Prayer

5 minutes: Wrap Up

  • How Does God Look After You? Gather everyone on the floor with the "How Does God Look After You?" page. Ask each child: "How does God look after you?" Give ideas if needed: food to eat, a family who loves me, a warm home, friends, keeping me safe. Write each answer with the child's name at the end of a line coming out from the middle circle, like a spider web. Hang it by the door so parents see it at pickup.
  • Wrap up: Remind the children that even when hard things happened to Joseph, God was with him the whole time and looked after him. Joseph got to know God, and God wants us to know him too! We can get to know God by talking to him, singing to him, and hearing his stories.
  • Closing Prayer: Play the Prayer Time video (about 1 minute), or lead it yourself: "Let's give ourselves a big hug, so we can feel how near God is to us." [Wrap your arms around yourself] "Dear God, thank you for loving us and for being near to us, just like you were with Joseph. Help us to know you more and more. In Jesus' name, Amen."
  • Pickup: Have the children wear their crowns to meet their parents! Give each parent a take-home card and a Key Verse colouring page.

10. Extra Activities (Only If You Have Time)

  • Disciple Tag: You are "it." Run around and chase the children! Keep it gentle with soft tags, and no outs.
  • Joseph's Coat Parade: Let each child take a turn wearing the colourful "coat" and spinning around while everyone cheers, "God is with you!"
