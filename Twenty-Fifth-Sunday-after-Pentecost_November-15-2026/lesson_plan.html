Sunday School Plan: Twenty-Fifth Sunday after Pentecost

Season After Pentecost
Scripture: 1 Samuel 16:1–13 (David the Shepherd Boy)
Theme: God Chooses David, God Looks at the Heart, Getting to Know God
Big Idea: God wants us to know him.
Key Verse: "Come near to God, and he will come near to you." James 4:8a
Series: Know (Week 3)

Master Materials List (for about 10 children)

  • Offering plate
  • Laptop with the Key Verse video (Desktop → Church → David (Know Week 3) → Videos). It's the same video as the Joseph week.
  • 8 brothers cut-outs (1 set, cut apart): 7 big brothers + David, the smallest one holding a shepherd's staff
  • Heart sign ("People look at the outside of a person...", 1 copy), sticky notes (about 12), a marker, and wall-safe tape
  • Shepherd and sheep pictures (5, optional). No printouts? Show them on your laptop instead: Desktop → Church → David (Know Week 3) → Printables → Wk 3 Shepherd and Sheep.
  • Take-home cards, 12 (3 sheets, cut into 4 cards each)
  • Any leftover November Key Verse colouring pages, for children who missed the Joseph week

There's no craft and no story video this week. You tell the story yourself using the 8 brothers, and the hands-on part is the brother hunt and the sticky-note heart sign.

Before Class (about 10 minutes)

  1. Cut apart the 8 brothers. Find David: he's the smallest one, holding a shepherd's staff.
  2. Hide David somewhere in the room (under a chair, behind a bin, or behind a sheep picture). Remember where!
  3. If you have the sheep pictures, tape them low on one wall. That's "the fields."
  4. Keep the 7 big brothers in a pile next to where you'll sit for the story.
  5. Cut the take-home sheets into 12 cards.
  6. On your laptop, open the Videos folder so the Key Verse video is ready.
  7. Put the heart sign, sticky notes, marker, and tape somewhere handy.

1. Introduction and Welcome

5 minutes: Weekend Check-In and "What's a Shepherd?"

Welcome everyone! Ask the children how their week was and let a few share. Then hold up the shepherd and sheep pictures (or show them on the laptop): "Who knows what this person's job is? He's a shepherd! A shepherd is someone who takes care of sheep."

Shepherd facts to share:

  • Shepherds work long days outside.
  • They walk a LOT with their sheep.
  • They work in sunshine, rain, and cold.
  • They keep their sheep safe from wild animals.
  • They make sure the sheep have food and water.

"Can everyone make a sheep sound? Baaa! Today we're going to meet a young shepherd boy from the Bible. His name is David! David knew God, and God wants us to know him too. But first, let's do our offering."

2. Offering and Prayer

1 minute: Offering

Collect the offering/donations while singing the money dropping song.

1 minute: Prayer over the Offering

"God, thank you that you know us and love us. We give you this gift today. Help us to know you more. In Jesus' name, Amen."

Transition: "Let's all sit on the carpet for our Bible story. I need lots of helpers!"

3. The Story: David the Shepherd Boy

10 minutes: Interactive Story with the 8 Brothers

The Story: (Sit with the children on the floor with the 7 big brothers next to you. David is already hidden somewhere in the room!)

"A long, long time ago, in a little town called Bethlehem, there lived a man named Jesse. Jesse had a BIG family. He had 8 sons! Let's count them." [Lay the 7 brothers on the floor one at a time and count together: 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 7...]

"Wait a minute... Jesse had 8 sons, but I only see 7! Hmm. Keep your eyes open. Maybe we'll find the last one later!"

"God had a very important job for a man named Samuel. God said, 'Samuel, go to Jesse's house. I have chosen one of his sons to be the next king!' So Samuel walked, and walked, and walked all the way to Bethlehem." [Everyone pats their knees like walking feet!]

"Samuel saw the oldest brother. He was tall and strong!" [Hold up the first brother.] "Samuel thought, 'He looks like a king. This must be him!' But God said, 'No. People look at the outside. But I look at the heart.'" [Everyone puts a hand on their heart.]

"So Samuel looked at the next brother... was it him?" [Hold up each brother one at a time. Everyone shakes their head and says, "Not him!" Do all 7.]

"Samuel was confused. He asked Jesse, 'Are these ALL your sons?' Jesse said, 'Well... there's one more. The youngest. He's out in the fields looking after the sheep.' Samuel said, 'Go get him!' Can you help me find him? Get your binoculars ready!" [Everyone makes binoculars with their hands and searches the room for David!]

"You found him! This is the youngest brother. His name is David! When David came in, God said, 'This is the one!' So Samuel poured special oil on David's head to show that God had chosen him to be the next king." [Everyone pretends to pour oil on their own head: "Drip, drip, drip!"]

"God didn't choose the tallest brother or the oldest brother. God looked at David's heart! And as David grew up, he got to know God more and more. God wants us to know him too!" [Everyone gives a big cheer for David!]

4. Questions and Reflections

5 minutes: Ask the children the following questions:

  • How many sons did Jesse have? (Answer: 8!)
  • Who did God send to find the new king? (Answer: Samuel.)
  • Samuel thought the tall, strong brother would be king. What did God say? (Answer: "I look at the heart.")
  • Where was David when Samuel came? (Answer: Out in the fields looking after the sheep.)
  • What did Samuel pour on David's head? (Answer: Oil, to show God chose him.)
  • David got to know God as he grew up. How can we get to know God? (Answer: Praying, hearing Bible stories, coming to church, singing to God.)

Transition: "Everybody stand up! Let's do our Bible verse."

5. Key Verse

3 minutes: Key Verse Video and Actions

Video: Desktop → Church → David (Know Week 3) → Videos → Key Verse Video with Paige and Lauren Know Early Years.mp4 (about 1½ minutes). It's the same video as the Joseph week, so the kids may remember the actions! Have everyone copy Paige and Lauren, then say it together one more time:

"Come near to God, and he will come near to you." James 4:8

(No video? Use these actions: "Come near" [wave your hand toward you] "to God" [point up] "and he will come near" [hug yourself] "to you!" [point to a friend])

Transition: "Stay standing! Did you know David loved to sing songs to God? Let's sing one too!"

6. Song

3 minutes: Worship Song

Play a worship song the kids love and dance together. David wrote lots of songs to God, and many of them are in the Bible!

Transition: "In our story, Samuel had to go way up into the hills to find David and his sheep. Let's go on an adventure and look for David!"

7. Game: Looking for David

5–10 minutes: Follow the Leader

Why: In the story, David was out in the hills with the sheep, and Samuel had to go find him.

How to Play:

  1. Everyone stands in a line behind you.
  2. Show them how to make binoculars: curl your hands into circles and hold them up to your eyes.
  3. "Let's go up the hills to find David!" Lead them around the room and they copy you: walk, tiptoe, march, hop, crawl like a sheep, and run on the spot.
  4. Every so often, stop: "Binoculars up! Do you see David?" Look around... "Not here! Keep going!"
  5. At the end, hold up the David cut-out: "There he is!"
  6. Let a few children take a turn being the leader. There are no "outs." Everyone keeps playing!

Say: "God wanted David to know him, and God wants us to know him too!"

Transition: "Let's sit down on the carpet and catch our breath. Now let's think about what's in our hearts!"

8. Group Activity: What's in My Heart?

10 minutes: Sticky-Note Heart Sign

Why: God didn't care how tall or strong David was. God looked at David's heart.

  1. Hold up the sign and read it out loud:

"People look at the outside of a person. But the Lord looks at what is in the heart."
1 Samuel 16:7

  1. "Samuel thought the tall, strong brother should be king. But God looked at David's heart! What do you think God sees when he looks in YOUR heart?"
  2. Give ideas if needed: love, kindness, sharing, helping, being a good friend, loving God.
  3. Write each child's answer and name on a sticky note (keep it short, like "Kind – Sam").
  4. Each child sticks their own note on the sign.
  5. Ask if anyone wants to say a one-sentence prayer, like "Thank you, God, for loving me!"
  6. Tape the sign by the door so parents see it at pickup.

Transition: "Let's put our hands on our hearts for our closing prayer."

9. Closing Prayer

2 minutes: Prayer (no video this week)

[Everyone puts their hands on their hearts.] "Dear God, thank you for looking at our hearts and loving us just the way we are. Help us to know you more and more, just like David did. In Jesus' name, Amen."

10. Pickup

  • Show parents the heart sign by the door!
  • Take-home cards are for parents. Each card has the Key Verse and 3 simple things to do at home this week: ask their child who the shepherd boy was, make a playlist of worship songs (because David wrote songs to God), and look up 1 Samuel 16:7 in the Bible together.
  • Give each parent 1 take-home card: "Today we learned about David the shepherd boy. Here's what to talk about at home this week!"
  • If a child missed the Joseph week, give them a November Key Verse colouring page too.

11. Extra Activities (Only If You Have Time)

  • Find David Again: Everyone covers their eyes while one child hides David somewhere in the room. Then everyone hunts for him with their binoculars! Take turns being the hider.
  • Sheep, Come Home!: The children are the sheep. They crawl around the room going "Baaa!" When you (the shepherd) call "Sheep, come home!", everyone crawls back and sits by you. Play again. No outs!
  • Disciple Tag: You are "it." Run around and chase the children! Keep it gentle with soft tags, and no outs.
