Sunday School Plan: Sixteenth Sunday after Pentecost

Season After Pentecost (Green)
Scripture: Matthew 18:21–35
Theme: Mercy, Forgiveness, The Unmerciful Servant

Master Materials List

  • Offering plate
  • A large bristol board (pre-divided into 4 sections using marker)
  • Markers or crayons for drawing
  • A phone or speaker to play music for the freeze dance game
  • Extra Sunday School Sheets and crayons/markers (for children who prefer to colour)

1. Introduction and Welcome

5 minutes: Weekend Check-In

Welcome everyone! We are in the green Season after Pentecost, learning how to follow Jesus. Ask the children how their week was and let a few share. Then preview the lesson: "Today we are learning about a question Peter asked Jesus: How many times do I have to forgive someone? Jesus told a story about mercy and forgiveness to answer him. Let's find out what it means! But first, let's do our offering."

2. Offering and Prayer

1 minute: Offering

Collect the offering/donations while singing the money dropping song.

1 minute: Prayer over the Offering

"God, thank you for showing us so much mercy and love. We offer this gift to you. Help us to hear your words today and learn to be forgiving. In Jesus' name, Amen."

3. The Story

5 minutes: Interactive Story

The Story: (Read this narratively to the children using the iPad App. Encourage them to act it out with you as you read!)

"One day, Peter asked Jesus, 'How many times should I forgive someone who does something wrong to me? Seven times?' Jesus smiled and said, 'No, Peter. Seventy-seven times!' which meant we should ALWAYS forgive." [Hold up seven fingers and look confused, just like Peter!]

"Jesus told a story: A king had a servant who owed him a massive amount of money. The servant begged, 'Please have mercy on me!' The king had a kind heart and totally forgave the huge debt!" [Put your hands together, bow your head, and pretend to beg for mercy!]

"But then, that same servant went and found a man who owed him just a tiny bit of money. Instead of forgiving him, he was super grumpy and mean, and he threw the poor man in jail!" [Cross your arms, stomp your foot, and show me your meanest, grumpiest face!]

"Jesus said that the king was very disappointed. Because God has shown us so much mercy and love, we must also show mercy and forgive our friends and family from the bottom of our hearts." [Run and give someone a big hug to show forgiveness and mercy!]

4. Questions and Reflections

5 minutes: Ask the children the following questions:

  • We are in the season after Pentecost when we learn how to follow Jesus. One day, Peter wanted to know how many times forgiveness should be given, so he asked. How did Jesus respond? (Answer: Seventy-seven times, meaning always!)
  • Jesus told Peter a story about mercy. Mercy is the compassionate treatment of someone suffering or in need. Imagine yourself watching the servant leave after his huge debt was forgiven. What do you think he might do next?
  • The servant received great mercy but struggled to share it. What makes it hard for people to offer kindness and forgiveness to others?
  • How are people and communities different when mercy is shared instead of not given?
  • What kinds of responses help people learn, grow, and begin again?

5. Bristol Board Drawing Activity

10 minutes: Drawing the Story Together

Transition: "Let's draw out the story of mercy on our bristol board!"

  • Section 1 (The Question): Have 1-2 children draw someone holding up seven fingers. Ask: "How many times did Jesus say to forgive?"
  • Section 2 (Mercy): Have 1-2 children draw a king forgiving someone. Ask: "What is mercy?"
  • Section 3 (Being Mean): Have 1-2 children draw someone with a grumpy face. Ask: "Why is it bad when we refuse to share mercy?"
  • Section 4 (Forgiveness): Have the rest draw people hugging and being kind. Ask: "How can we show forgiveness to others?"

6. Reading the Scripture

2 minutes: Matthew 18:21–35

Transition: "Now let's read the actual words from the Gospel of Matthew about mercy and forgiveness!"

Then Peter came and said to him, "Lord, if another member of the church sins against me, how often should I forgive? As many as seven times?" Jesus said to him, "Not seven times, but, I tell you, seventy-seven times.

...And out of pity for him, the lord of that slave released him and forgave him the debt. But that same slave, as he went out, came upon one of his fellow slaves who owed him a hundred denarii; and seizing him by the throat, he said, 'Pay what you owe.'

...Then his lord summoned him and said to him, 'You wicked slave! I forgave you all that debt because you pleaded with me. Should you not have had mercy on your fellow slave, as I had mercy on you?'"

7. Game / Activity

20 minutes: "Jesus Says" Freeze Dance

(Note: Any children who don't want to participate can colour using the extra Sunday School sheets!)

Intro: "We are playing freeze dance with words from today's story! When I say 'Jesus says' before the word, you do the action. If I just say the word without it, you stay still. If you move, you are out for that round but back in the next one!"

The 4 Calls:

  • "Question!" Hold up seven fingers and look confused!
  • "Mercy!" Put your hands together, bow your head, and pretend to beg for mercy!
  • "No Way!" Cross your arms, stomp your foot, and show a grumpy face!
  • "Forgive!" Don't freeze! Run and give someone a big hug or a high five!

How to Play (Jesus Says rules):

  1. Play upbeat music and let the children dance freely around the room.
  2. Stop the music. Only do the action if you say "Jesus says..." first. If you just say the word, they should stay still.
  3. Anyone who does the action without the prompt is out for that round and back in the next.
  4. Mix up speed, order, and whether you use the prompt to keep them guessing.
  5. At the end, ask: "Which of these actions was the most fun to do?"

8. Wrap Up and Prayer

5 minutes: Wrap Up

  • Wrap up: Remind the children that God has forgiven us for all of our mistakes and showed us incredible mercy. Because we have been forgiven so much, we should always try to forgive others when they make mistakes, sharing that same mercy with them.
  • Closing Prayer: "God, thank you for your endless mercy and for forgiving us when we make mistakes. Please help us to have soft hearts, so we can be quick to forgive our friends and family too. We pray this in Jesus' name, Amen."
