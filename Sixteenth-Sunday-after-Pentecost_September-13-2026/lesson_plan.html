Sunday School Plan: Sixteenth Sunday after Pentecost

Season After Pentecost (Green)
Scripture: Matthew 18:21–35
Theme: Mercy, Forgiveness, The Unmerciful Servant

Master Materials List

  • Offering plate
  • A large bristol board (pre-divided into 4 sections using marker)
  • Markers or crayons for drawing
  • A phone or speaker to play music for the freeze dance game
  • Extra Sunday School Sheets and crayons/markers (for children who prefer to colour)

1. Introduction and Welcome

5 minutes: Weekend Check-In

Welcome everyone! We are in the green Season after Pentecost, learning how to follow Jesus. Ask the children how their first week of school was and let a few share. Then preview the lesson: "Today we are learning about a question Peter asked Jesus: How many times do I have to forgive someone? Jesus told a story about mercy and forgiveness to answer him. Let's find out what it means! But first, let's do our offering."

2. Offering and Prayer

1 minute: Offering

Collect the offering/donations while singing the money dropping song.

1 minute: Prayer over the Offering

Say a short opening prayer and a prayer over the offering.

3. The Story

5 minutes: Interactive Story

The Story: (Read this narratively to the children using the iPad. Encourage them to act it out with you as you read!)

"One day, Peter asked Jesus, 'How many times should I forgive someone who does something wrong to me? Seven times?' Jesus smiled and said, 'No, Peter. Seventy-seven times!' which meant we should ALWAYS forgive." [Hold up seven fingers and do it twice to show seventy-seven!]

"Jesus told a story: A king had a servant who owed him a massive amount of money. The servant begged, 'Please have mercy on me!' The king had a kind heart and totally forgave the huge debt!" [Put your hands together and pretend to beg for mercy!]

"But then, that same servant went and found a man who owed him just a tiny bit of money. Instead of forgiving him, he was super grumpy and mean, and he threw the poor man in jail!" [Cover your eyes with your hands and bow your head like you are in jail!]

"Jesus said that the king was very disappointed. Because God has shown us so much mercy and love, we must also show mercy and forgive our friends and family from the bottom of our hearts." [Give someone next to you a handshake to show forgiveness and mercy!]

Video: Play this video on the iPad or laptop: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ED3fzkS-bhA

4. Questions and Reflections

5 minutes: Ask the children the following questions:

  • Peter wanted to know how many times forgiveness should be given, so he asked. How did Jesus respond? (Answer: Seventy-seven times, meaning always!)
  • The servant received great mercy but struggled to share it. What makes it hard for people to offer kindness and forgiveness to others?
  • You're now in a new grade in school and sometimes you might have to show forgiveness. What might that look like?

5. Bristol Board Drawing Activity

10 minutes: Drawing the Story Together

Transition: "Let's draw out the story of mercy on our bristol board!"

  • Section 1 (77): Have 1-2 children draw someone holding up seven fingers. Ask: "How many times did Jesus say to forgive?"
  • Section 2 (Mercy): Have 1-2 children draw a king forgiving someone.
  • Section 3 (Being Mean): Have 1-2 children draw someone with a grumpy face. Ask: "Why is it bad when we refuse to share mercy?"
  • Section 4 (Forgiveness): Have the rest draw people hugging and being kind. Ask: "How can we show forgiveness to others?"

6. Game / Activity

10 minutes: "Jesus Says" Freeze Dance

(Note: Any children who don't want to participate can colour using the extra Sunday School sheets!)

Intro: "We are playing freeze dance with words from today's story! Dance while the music plays, but when the music stops, listen carefully! I will say 'Jesus says' followed by a word from our story, and we will all do the action together!"

The 4 Calls:

  • "77!" Hold up seven fingers and do it twice!
  • "Mercy!" Put your hands together and pretend to beg for mercy!
  • "Jail!" Cover your eyes with your hands and bow your head!
  • "Forgive!" Don't freeze! Give someone next to you a handshake!

How to Play:

  1. Play upbeat music and let the children dance freely around the room.
  2. Stop the music. Say "Jesus says..." followed by one of the calls, and have everyone do the action together.
  3. There are no "outs"—everyone keeps playing and having fun!
  4. Mix up the speed and order of the calls to keep it exciting.
  5. At the end, ask: "Which of these actions was the most fun to do?"

7. Wrap Up and Prayer

5 minutes: Wrap Up

  • Wrap up: Remind the children that God has forgiven us for all of our mistakes and showed us incredible mercy. Because we have been forgiven so much, we should always try to forgive others when they make mistakes, sharing that same mercy with them.
  • Closing Prayer: Say a closing prayer.
