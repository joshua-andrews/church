One day, Peter asked Jesus, "How many times should I forgive someone who does something wrong to me? Seven times?" Jesus smiled and said, "No, Peter. Seventy-seven times!" which meant we should ALWAYS forgive.
Jesus told a story: A king had a servant who owed him a massive amount of money. The servant begged, "Please have mercy on me!" The king had a kind heart and totally forgave the huge debt!
But then, that same servant went and found a man who owed him just a tiny bit of money. Instead of forgiving him, he was super grumpy and mean, and he threw the poor man in jail!
Jesus said that the king was very disappointed. Because God has shown us so much mercy and love, we must also show mercy and forgive our friends and family from the bottom of our hearts.
