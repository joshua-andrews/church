Sunday School Plan: Fifth Sunday after Pentecost

Season After Pentecost (Green)
Scripture: Matthew 10:40–42
Theme: Welcoming Others, A Cup of Cold Water, Small Acts of Kindness

Master Materials List

  • Offering plate
  • A large bristol board (pre-divided into 4 sections using marker)
  • Markers or crayons for drawing
  • A phone or speaker to play music for the freeze dance game
  • Extra Sunday School Sheets and crayons/markers (for children who prefer to colour)

1. Introduction and Welcome

5 minutes: Weekend Check-In

Welcome everyone! We are still in the green Season after Pentecost, learning how to grow God's love. Ask the children how their week was and let a few share. Then preview the lesson: "Today we are learning about a time Jesus talked about helping people. He told a story where the biggest, most important thing someone did was just give someone a cup of cold water! But first, let's do our offering."

2. Offering and Prayer

1 minute: Offering

Collect the offering/donations while singing the money dropping song.

1 minute: Prayer over the Offering

"God, thank you for noticing every small thing we do to help others. Thank you for welcoming us, and teaching us how to welcome our friends and strangers. We offer this gift to you. Help us to hear your words today. In Jesus' name, Amen."

3. The Story

5 minutes: Interactive Story

The Story: (Read this narratively to the children using the iPad App. Encourage them to act it out with you as you read!)

"Jesus was teaching his disciples about how important it is to be a good host and a good helper. He told them, 'Whoever welcomes you, welcomes me. And whoever welcomes me, welcomes God.'" [Open your arms wide like you're giving a big hug. That's what it means to welcome someone!]

"Jesus explained that if someone comes to your town—like a prophet or a righteous person who is traveling—and you open your door to them and give them a place to rest, God sees that. And God will reward you for your kindness!" [Pretend you are opening a heavy door and waving someone inside your house to rest.]

"Jesus then said something amazing: 'And whoever gives even a cup of cold water to one of these little ones... truly I tell you, none of these will lose their reward.'" [Make your hands into the shape of a cup and pretend to take a long drink of cold water. Ahhhh!]

"A cup of cold water might seem like a very small thing. But Jesus wanted them to know that every single act of kindness matters to God. No matter how small you are, or how small your help seems, God notices and loves it when you care for others." [Give the person next to you a big high five. God notices when you are a good helper!]

4. Questions and Reflections

5 minutes: Ask the children the following questions:

  • The season after Pentecost is a time when we learn how everyday acts of care can help grow God’s love in the world. What helping words can you remember from the story? (Answer: Welcome, cup of cold water, care, reward, help.)
  • Our story focuses on the idea of welcome. When have you been welcomed by someone you didn’t know well? When have you welcomed someone you didn’t know well?
  • One way to be a good helper is to be a good noticer. If someone needs help, what signs do you think you might watch for? (Answer: If they look sad, if they are crying, if they are struggling to carry something, if they are sitting alone.)
  • The example of offering a cold cup of water is very simple. What simple thing could you do to care for, welcome, or help someone? (Answer: Hold a door, say hello, share a toy, sit with someone at lunch, draw a picture for them.)
  • Who in your community is really good at welcoming others? What makes them so good at it?

5. Bristol Board Drawing Activity

10 minutes: Drawing the Story Together

Transition: "Let's draw out the ways we can welcome people on our bristol board!"

  • Section 1 (Welcoming Jesus): Have 1-2 children draw someone giving a big hug. Ask: "Who are we welcoming when we welcome others?"
  • Section 2 (Opening the Door): Have 1-2 children draw a house with an open door and a traveler coming in. Ask: "How can we make people feel at home?"
  • Section 3 (Cup of Water): Have 1-2 children draw a cup of cold water with ice cubes in it. Ask: "Why is a cup of water a big deal when you are thirsty?"
  • Section 4 (God's Reward): Have the rest draw a big smiling face in the sky looking down. Ask: "Who notices when we do something kind, even if no one else sees it?"

6. Reading the Scripture

2 minutes: Matthew 10:40–42

Transition: "Now let's read the actual words from the Gospel of Matthew about the cup of cold water!"

"Whoever welcomes you welcomes me, and whoever welcomes me welcomes the one who sent me. Whoever welcomes a prophet in the name of a prophet will receive a prophet’s reward, and whoever welcomes a righteous person in the name of a righteous person will receive the reward of the righteous, and whoever gives even a cup of cold water to one of these little ones in the name of a disciple—truly I tell you, none of these will lose their reward."

7. Game / Activity

20 minutes: "Jesus Says" Freeze Dance

(Note: Any children who don't want to participate can colour using the extra Sunday School sheets!)

Intro: "We are playing freeze dance with words from today's story! When I say 'Jesus says' before the word, you do the action. If I just say the word without it, you stay still. If you move, you are out for that round but back in the next one!"

The 5 Calls:

  • "Welcome!" Open your arms wide for a big hug.
  • "Prophet!" Open an imaginary door and wave someone in.
  • "Water!" Cup your hands and take a loud, refreshing slurp.
  • "Notice!" Make binoculars with your hands and look around for someone to help.
  • "Reward!" Don't freeze! Run and give everyone around you a high five.

How to Play (Jesus Says rules):

  1. Play upbeat music and let the children dance freely around the room.
  2. Stop the music. Only do the action if you say "Jesus says..." first. If you just say the word, they should stay still.
  3. Anyone who does the action without the prompt is out for that round and back in the next.
  4. Mix up speed, order, and whether you use the prompt to keep them guessing.
  5. At the end, ask: "Which of these actions was the easiest to remember?"

8. Wrap Up and Prayer

5 minutes: Wrap Up

  • Wrap up: Remind the children that you don't have to be a grown-up or do something huge to be a helper. Sometimes the best help is just opening a door, saying hello, or giving someone a cup of water when they are thirsty. Every single act of kindness matters, and God smiles when we care for each other.
  • Closing Prayer: "God, thank you for teaching us how to love. Help us to be good noticers this week, so we can see when people need help. Help us to be brave enough to offer a cup of cold water or a kind word. Thank you for noticing and loving us. In Jesus' name, Amen."
