Jesus was teaching his disciples about how important it is to be a good host and a good helper. He told them, "Whoever welcomes you, welcomes me. And whoever welcomes me, welcomes God."
* Open your arms wide like you're giving a big hug. That's what it means to welcome someone!
Jesus explained that if someone comes to your town—like a prophet or a righteous person who is traveling—and you open your door to them and give them a place to rest, God sees that. And God will reward you for your kindness!
* Pretend you are opening a heavy door and waving someone inside your house to rest.
Jesus then said something amazing: "And whoever gives even a cup of cold water to one of these little ones... truly I tell you, none of these will lose their reward."
* Make your hands into the shape of a cup and pretend to take a long drink of cold water. Ahhhh!
A cup of cold water might seem like a very small thing. But Jesus wanted them to know that every single act of kindness matters to God. No matter how small you are, or how small your help seems, God notices and loves it when you care for others.
* Give the person next to you a big high five. God notices when you are a good helper!
