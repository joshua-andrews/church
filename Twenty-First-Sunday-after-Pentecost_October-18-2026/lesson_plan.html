Sunday School Plan: Twenty-First Sunday after Pentecost

Season After Pentecost (Green)
Scripture: Genesis 28:10–22 (Jacob's Dream)
Theme: God Keeps His Promises, Jacob's Stairway, God Is With Us
Key Verse: "God has made a great many promises." 2 Corinthians 1:20a
Series: Promises (Week 4 of 5)

Master Materials List

  • Offering plate
  • Printed Jacob's Dream flipbook (Naomi printed this)
  • Jacob's Stairway colouring pages, 1 per child (Naomi printed these)
  • Gold paper, pre-cut into small strips (about 1.5" long × ½" wide), about 10 per child
  • Glue sticks and crayons
  • iPad or laptop with the Storytime video downloaded
  • A Bible (open to Genesis 28)
  • A phone or speaker to play music for the freeze dance game
  • Masking tape (optional, for the stairway in the game)
  • "What is God's Dream for Me?" poster and a marker (optional)
  • Take-home cards (Naomi printed these)
  • Key Verse colouring pages and crayons (for children who prefer to colour)

1. Introduction and Welcome

5 minutes: Weekend Check-In

Welcome everyone! We are still in the green Season after Pentecost. Ask the children how their week was and let a few share. Then ask: "Did anyone have a dream last night? What was it about?" Let a few share. Then preview the lesson: "Today we are learning about a man named Jacob who had a VERY special dream. In his dream, God made him a big promise. Let's find out what it was! But first, let's do our offering."

2. Offering and Prayer

1 minute: Offering

Collect the offering/donations while singing the money dropping song.

1 minute: Prayer over the Offering

"God, thank you for always keeping your promises. We give you this gift today. Help us to listen and learn about you. In Jesus' name, Amen."

3. The Story

5 minutes: Interactive Story

The Story: (Sit the children on the floor. Hold up the matching picture from the printed Jacob's Dream flipbook as you read each part. Encourage them to act it out with you!)

"Jacob was going on a long, long trip. When the sun went down, he was very tired. There was no bed and no pillow, so Jacob found a big stone, put it under his head, and went to sleep!" [Lay your head on your hands like a stone pillow and pretend to snore!]

"While Jacob was sleeping, he had an amazing dream. He saw a giant stairway that went from the ground all the way up into heaven! And there were angels going up and down, up and down the stairs!" [Climb the stairs in place with your knees up high, then climb back down!]

"At the very top of the stairway was God! God said, 'Jacob, I am with you. I will take care of you wherever you go. Your family will grow so big that it will spread out everywhere. And I will do everything I have promised you.'" [Give a big thumbs up and shout, "God keeps his promises!"]

"When Jacob woke up, he said, 'Wow! God is here, in this very place, and I didn't even know it!' Jacob stood his stone pillow up tall, poured oil on top to show it was a special place, and named it Bethel, which means 'House of God.'" [Jump up with big wide eyes and shout, "WOW! God is here!"]

"Then Jacob prayed. He asked God to stay with him and take care of him, and he said, 'You will be my God.' And God kept every single promise he made to Jacob, because God ALWAYS keeps his promises!" [Fold your hands and bow your head like Jacob praying!]

Video: Play the 3-minute "Storytime: God's Promise to Jacob" video on the iPad or laptop. It's in the Church folder under Videos → Storytime Gods Promise to Jacob.mp4. Download it to the device before class so it plays without Wi-Fi.

"We're going to watch the story of Jacob's dream now. Let's all get comfortable and watch together!"

4. Questions and Reflections

5 minutes: Ask the children the following questions:

  • Who is the story about? (Answer: Jacob!)
  • What did Jacob use for a pillow? (Answer: A stone!)
  • What did Jacob see in his dream? (Answer: A giant stairway to heaven with angels going up and down!)
  • What did God promise Jacob? (Answer: That God would be with him and take care of him wherever he went.)
  • What did Jacob say when he woke up? (Answer: "God is here!")
  • What are some places you go during the week? Is God with you there too? (Answer: Yes! God is with us everywhere, because God keeps his promises.)

5. Craft: Jacob's Stairway

10 minutes: Golden Stairway Colouring Page

Transition: "Let's make our very own golden stairway to heaven, just like the one in Jacob's dream!"

(Before class: cut the gold paper into small strips, about 1.5" long and ½" wide. If you don't have gold paper, yellow paper or yellow crayons work fine!)

  1. Give each child a Jacob's Stairway colouring page, a glue stick, and a small pile of gold strips.
  2. Put the crayons in the middle of the table where everyone can reach.
  3. Have them glue one gold strip onto each step of the stairway. Help younger ones by guiding their hands.
  4. Then they colour in the rest of the picture: Jacob, the angels, and the sky.
  5. While they work, ask: "Who was at the top of the stairway?" (God!) and "What does God always do with his promises?" (He keeps them!)
  6. Write each child's name on their page so they can take it home at pickup.

6. Reading the Scripture

2 minutes: Genesis 28:10–16

Transition: "Now let's read the real words from the Bible! This story is in the very first book of the Bible, called Genesis." (Hold up the Bible open to Genesis 28 so the children can see it.)

"Jacob left Beer-sheba and went toward Haran. He came to a certain place and stayed there for the night, because the sun had set. Taking one of the stones of the place, he put it under his head and lay down in that place.

And he dreamed that there was a ladder set up on the earth, the top of it reaching to heaven; and the angels of God were ascending and descending on it. And the LORD stood beside him...

...'Know that I am with you and will keep you wherever you go... for I will not leave you until I have done what I have promised you.' Then Jacob woke from his sleep and said, 'Surely the LORD is in this place—and I did not know it!'"

7. Game / Activity

10 minutes: "Jacob's Stairway" Freeze Dance

(Note: Any children who don't want to participate can colour using the Key Verse colouring pages!)

(Optional: If you have masking tape, make two ladder shapes on the floor side by side, one for going up and one for coming down. Kids can dance up one stairway and down the other!)

Intro: "We are playing freeze dance with words from today's story! Dance while the music plays, but when the music stops, listen carefully! I will say 'Jacob says' followed by a word from our story, and we will all do the action together!"

The 4 Calls:

  • "Sleep!" Lay your head on your hands like a stone pillow and pretend to snore!
  • "Stairway!" Climb the stairs in place with your knees up high, then climb back down!
  • "God is here!" Jump up with big wide eyes and shout, "WOW!"
  • "Promise!" Don't freeze! Give a big thumbs up and shout, "God keeps his promises!"

How to Play:

  1. Play upbeat music and let the children dance freely around the room.
  2. Stop the music. Say "Jacob says..." followed by one of the calls, and have everyone do the action together.
  3. There are no "outs"—everyone keeps playing and having fun!
  4. Mix up the speed and order of the calls to keep it exciting.
  5. At the end, ask: "Which of these actions was the most fun to do?"

8. Wrap Up and Prayer

5 minutes: Wrap Up

  • Wrap up: Remind the children that God made a promise to Jacob to be with him wherever he went, and God kept it. God is with us too: at home, at school, at the park, everywhere we go! We know this because God always keeps his promises.
  • Key Verse: Say it together with actions, two or three times: "God [point up to the sky] has made a great [jump] many [jump] promises!" [jump] 2 Corinthians 1:20 [open your hands like a book]
  • God's Dream for Me (optional, if you have the poster): Show the "What is God's Dream for Me?" poster. Ask: "What do you think God's dream for you is?" Give ideas if needed: being kind to friends, sharing, helping at home, talking to God every day. Write each child's answer and name on the stairs of the poster, then hang it by the door so parents see it at pickup.
  • Closing Prayer: "Dear God, thank you for keeping all of your promises. Thank you for being with us everywhere we go, just like you were with Jacob. Help us remember that you are always with us this week. In Jesus' name, Amen."
  • Pickup: Give each parent a take-home card and make sure every child takes their stairway craft home.

9. Extra Activities (Only If You Have Time)

  • Draw a Dream (good while kids are arriving): Put out blank paper and markers. Ask the children to draw a favourite dream, or something they'd love to dream about. Tell them today's story is about a dream God gave Jacob.
  • Mixed-Up Story: Lay the 5 story pictures on the floor in the wrong order. Have the children work together to put them in the right order, then retell the story. Mix them up and play again!
