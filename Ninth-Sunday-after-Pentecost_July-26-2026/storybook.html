We all have big, difficult feelings sometimes. Sometimes we feel so sad, scared, or confused that we don't even know what to say or how to pray to God about it.
* Show me what it's like when you're upset! Can you stomp your feet and make your grumpiest face?
But God sends the Holy Spirit to help us! The Bible says that when we don't have the words, the Holy Spirit prays for us with "groanings too deep for words." God understands exactly what is in our hearts.
* Take a deep breath and let out a big sigh. That's what it sounds like when the Holy Spirit helps our hearts feel settled.
Paul asked a huge question: "Who will separate us from God's love?" He said our connection to God is completely unbreakable! Just like a tight family hug, nothing in the whole world can ever pull us away from God.
* Lock your fingers together as tight as you can and try to pull them apart! We are unbreakable!
Because God loves us so much, Paul says we are "more than victorious!" That means even when things are really hard, we can cheer because we already have the victory through Jesus.
* Throw your arms up in a "V" shape and cheer like you just won a huge race!
