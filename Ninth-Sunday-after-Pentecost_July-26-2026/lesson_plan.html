Sunday School Plan: Ninth Sunday after Pentecost

Season After Pentecost (Green)
Scripture: Romans 8:26–39
Theme: Big Feelings, The Holy Spirit, Inseparable Love

Master Materials List

  • Offering plate
  • A large bristol board (pre-divided into 4 sections using marker)
  • Markers or crayons for drawing
  • A phone or speaker to play music for the freeze dance game
  • Extra Sunday School Sheets and crayons/markers (for children who prefer to colour)

1. Introduction and Welcome

5 minutes: Weekend Check-In

Welcome everyone! We are still in the green Season after Pentecost. Ask the children how their week was and let a few share. Then preview the lesson: "Today we are learning about a letter from Paul. He wrote about what happens when we have really big, difficult feelings, and what God does to help us. He also asks a huge question: Can ANYTHING separate us from God's love? Let's find out! But first, let's do our offering."

2. Offering and Prayer

1 minute: Offering

Collect the offering/donations while singing the money dropping song.

1 minute: Prayer over the Offering

"God, thank you for loving us no matter what. Thank you for sending the Holy Spirit to help us when we don't even know what to say. We offer this gift to you. Help us to hear your words today. In Jesus' name, Amen."

3. The Story

5 minutes: Interactive Story

The Story: (Read this narratively to the children using the iPad App. Encourage them to act it out with you as you read!)

"We all have big, difficult feelings sometimes. Sometimes we feel so sad, scared, or confused that we don't even know what to say or how to pray to God about it." [Show me what it's like when you're upset! Can you stomp your feet and make your grumpiest face?]

"But God sends the Holy Spirit to help us! The Bible says that when we don't have the words, the Holy Spirit prays for us with 'groanings too deep for words.' God understands exactly what is in our hearts." [Take a deep breath and let out a big sigh. That's what it sounds like when the Holy Spirit helps our hearts feel settled.]

"Paul asked a huge question: 'Who will separate us from God's love?' He said our connection to God is completely unbreakable! Just like a tight family hug, nothing in the whole world can ever pull us away from God." [Lock your fingers together as tight as you can and try to pull them apart! We are unbreakable!]

"Because God loves us so much, Paul says we are 'more than victorious!' That means even when things are really hard, we can cheer because we already have the victory through Jesus." [Throw your arms up in a "V" shape and cheer like you just won a huge race!]

4. Questions and Reflections

5 minutes: Ask the children the following questions:

  • In this season of Pentecost, we focus on learning how God is always with us. What does this scripture say cannot separate us from God? (Answer: Nothing! Not life, death, angels, the present, the future... nothing in all creation.)
  • This scripture tells us God loves and understands us with all of the big, difficult feelings we have. What are some examples of big, difficult feelings? (Answer: Anger, sadness, jealousy, frustration, grief.)
  • When you have felt big, difficult feelings, who or what helped you feel settled again?
  • This passage tells us we’re part of a big family with Jesus. What is good about being a part of a community or family?
  • God sends the Holy Spirit to help us care for one another as a community. How can your community show up for and help people who are struggling with big, difficult feelings?

5. Bristol Board Drawing Activity

10 minutes: Drawing the Story Together

Transition: "Let's draw out the ways we can welcome people on our bristol board!"

  • Section 1 (Big Feelings): Have 1-2 children draw someone looking angry or sad. Ask: "Is it okay to be sad sometimes?"
  • Section 2 (The Holy Spirit): Have 1-2 children draw a warm light or a dove representing the Holy Spirit comforting them. Ask: "Who prays for us when we don't have the words?"
  • Section 3 (God's Family): Have 1-2 children draw a group of people holding hands. Ask: "Who can we help when they are having big feelings?"
  • Section 4 (Nothing Can Separate Us): Have the rest draw a big, strong bridge or something connecting us to God. Ask: "What can break our connection to God's love?" (Nothing!)

6. Reading the Scripture

2 minutes: Romans 8:26–39

Transition: "Now let's read the actual words from the Letter to the Romans about nothing separating us from God's love!"

"Likewise the Spirit helps us in our weakness, for we do not know how to pray as we ought, but that very Spirit intercedes with groanings too deep for words. And God, who searches hearts, knows what is the mind of the Spirit...

...Who will separate us from the love of Christ? Will affliction or distress or persecution or famine or nakedness or peril or sword?

No, in all these things we are more than victorious through him who loved us. For I am convinced that neither death, nor life, nor angels, nor rulers, nor things present, nor things to come, nor powers, nor height, nor depth, nor anything else in all creation will be able to separate us from the love of God in Christ Jesus our Lord."

7. Game / Activity

20 minutes: "Jesus Says" Freeze Dance

(Note: Any children who don't want to participate can colour using the extra Sunday School sheets!)

Intro: "We are playing freeze dance with words from today's story! When I say 'Jesus says' before the word, you do the action. If I just say the word without it, you stay still. If you move, you are out for that round but back in the next one!"

The 4 Calls:

  • "Upset!" Stomp your feet and make your grumpiest face!
  • "Deep Breath!" Put your hands on your heart, take a huge breath in, and let out a big sigh!
  • "Unbreakable!" Lock your fingers together as tight as you can and try to pull them apart!
  • "Victory!" Don't freeze! Throw your arms up in a "V" shape and cheer like you just won a race!

How to Play (Jesus Says rules):

  1. Play upbeat music and let the children dance freely around the room.
  2. Stop the music. Only do the action if you say "Jesus says..." first. If you just say the word, they should stay still.
  3. Anyone who does the action without the prompt is out for that round and back in the next.
  4. Mix up speed, order, and whether you use the prompt to keep them guessing.
  5. At the end, ask: "Which of these actions was the most fun to do?"

8. Wrap Up and Prayer

5 minutes: Wrap Up

  • Wrap up: Remind the children that big, difficult feelings are completely normal, and even grown-ups have them. When we have them, we don't have to face them alone. The Holy Spirit is there to comfort us and pray for us, our community is there to hug us, and no matter what happens, NOTHING can ever take God's love away from us.
  • Closing Prayer: "God, thank you for loving us even when we are grumpy or sad. Thank you for the Holy Spirit that helps us feel settled. Help us to remember this week that absolutely nothing in all creation can separate us from your love. We pray this in Jesus' name, Amen."
